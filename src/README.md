# 🚨 CampusSOS

### Role-Based Campus Issue Reporting & Management System

CampusSOS is a role-based campus issue reporting and tracking platform designed to make campus complaint management simple, organized, and accountable.

Students can privately report campus issues and track the progress of their own complaints.

Management can view reports across the campus, search and filter them, assign priority, update their status, and remove unnecessary or duplicate reports.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Students often face problems such as:

- Water leakage
- Electrical issues
- Damaged infrastructure
- Classroom problems
- Cleanliness issues
- Safety concerns
- Facility maintenance problems

When complaints are handled through scattered channels, students may not know whether their issue has been received, reviewed, or resolved.

CampusSOS provides a centralized interface for reporting and tracking these issues.

---

# 👥 User Roles

CampusSOS has two separate user roles.

## 🎓 Student

Students can:

- Log in using their student roll number
- Create a campus issue report
- Add title, location, and description
- View only their own reports
- Search their own reports
- Track report status
- View assigned priority

Students cannot:

- View other students' reports
- Assign priority
- Change report status
- Delete reports

---

## 🏢 Management

Management can:

- Log in through the management portal
- View all campus reports
- Search reports
- Filter reports by status
- Filter reports by priority
- View complete report details
- Assign High, Medium, or Low priority
- Change report status
- Track resolution progress
- Delete unnecessary or duplicate reports
